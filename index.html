AI PopUp

A personal tool for one creator: it helps plan, make and publish short videos about AI and technology for the AI PopUp YouTube channel and its Instagram account.

What the app does

  • Runs only on the channel owner's own computer. There is no public sign-up and no server.
  • Uploads the owner's finished Shorts and their cover images to the owner's own YouTube channel, and Reels to the owner's own Instagram account, after the owner approves each one.
  • Reads the channel's own videos, comments and view statistics so the owner can see what works and answer comments. Every reply is approved by the owner before it is posted.

Who uses it

Only the owner of the AI PopUp channel. The app signs in with Google (YouTube) and Instagram only to act on the owner's own accounts.

Your data

How the app handles account data, and how to remove it, is described in the Privacy Policy. Use of the app is covered by the Terms of Service.
