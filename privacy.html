Privacy Policy

Effective 14 October 2026

AI PopUp ("the app") is a personal tool. Its only user is the owner of the AI PopUp YouTube channel and Instagram account, and it runs only on that owner's own Windows computer. This policy explains what account data the app uses, where it is kept, who it is shared with, and how to remove it.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What the app accesses

With Google sign-in, on the owner's own YouTube channel only:

  • youtube.upload: upload the owner's approved Shorts and set their cover images (thumbnails).
  • youtube.readonly: read the channel name and the channel's own videos and their status.
  • youtube.force-ssl: read comments on the channel's videos and post replies the owner has approved.
  • yt-analytics.readonly: read view statistics of the channel's own videos (for example views and how viewers found them).

With Instagram sign-in, on the owner's own professional Instagram account only: instagram_business_basic, instagram_business_content_publish (publish approved Reels and their covers), instagram_business_manage_comments (read comments and post approved replies) and instagram_business_manage_insights (read post statistics).

The app does not read data of other people's channels or accounts beyond the public comments left on the owner's own videos.

Where the data is kept

  • Everything stays on the owner's computer: a local database and local files. The app has no server and no cloud account of its own.
  • Sign-in tokens are stored encrypted with Windows Data Protection (DPAPI) for the owner's Windows user.
  • Backups are copies of that data kept on drives the owner chooses.

Who it is shared with

  • Google / YouTube and Instagram: only to carry out what the owner asks (upload, publish, read statistics, read and reply to comments).
  • The AI service the owner configures (for example Google Gemini): comment text and summaries of how past videos performed can be sent so the app can draft replies and video scripts. Nothing is posted without the owner's approval.
  • Telegram: status notifications about the app's own jobs, sent only to the owner's own Telegram chat.
  • When a Reel is published, its video and cover image are made available to Instagram for a short time through a temporary link so Instagram can fetch them.

Data is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with anyone else.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google APIs is used only to provide the features above to the owner.

Keeping and deleting data

  • Data is kept on the owner's computer until the owner deletes it.
  • To stop access at any time: in the app, Publishing → Disconnect & revoke; or remove the app's access in your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions (also reachable via security.google.com/settings/security/permissions), and for Instagram under Settings → Apps and websites.
  • Deleting the app's data folder on the owner's computer removes all stored account data, tokens and statistics. Revoked or expired tokens cannot be used again.

Data deletion requests

To ask for deletion of any data the app holds about you (for example a comment you left on an AI PopUp video), email gaurav11.pal@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion". It will be removed from the app within 30 days and you will get a reply when it is done.

Children

The app is a tool for its adult owner and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
